# Provisional coordinated lighting candidate

`../../pilot-lighting-refined-candidate.example.json` is an **opt-in, unreviewed** configuration for future matched and natural-mixture screens. It preserves the existing balanced candidate, scene and material controls, camera and 5 mm pad. Every scene context has a 5:4:1 mixture of broad, soft and original lamp settings, and the three contexts have equal total weight. All member weights are editable. The 10% original component preserves the original distributions; it is not a measurement or guarantee that 10% of rendered images are dark, glaring or intense.

The broad option favors area lights, raises and spreads possible lamp positions, broadens emitters and spot spread, and samples power over 300–1000 W. The soft option uses 80% of the original power range, larger lights and wider spot spread. Both soften spot edges. These coordinated settings are hypotheses, not established improvements; stronger lamps or greater height may have unpredictable interactions with geometry and indoor correction. Earlier reviews found blanket dimming darkened some already dim targets, and broadening alone did not eliminate pools. Ambient/sun-driven images may show little or no change.
