# Surface integration round three

Approved in the 2026-10-26 planning interview. Build on the Blender execution work in PR #78.

The shared comparison core preserves artwork, source hashes, count/size coverage intent and annotation
conventions; exact paired controls live within each engine, not across renderer pixels. Twenty independent
reference intents replace the earlier three-layout feature pilot. Specialist recipes may depart into
explicitly described niches; keep their results separate from core comparisons. Real capture calibration,
training and detector-transfer evidence remain later work (#59/#61/#62/#63/#64).

## Delivery agreement

- Shared deterministic wear fields feed unchanged 2D RGB wear and registered 3D albedo/roughness/bump
  maps. Ordinary recipes keep artwork recognizable; heavy wear has a separate stress role.
- Mainline panels remain flat, rigid, opaque and nominally 1 m / 1 x .75 m with 3 mm thickness on planar
  support. Expose independent pad/ground normals, coherent ground UVs, HDR/light and contextual caster
  controls. Keep all tuning and hardware values in saved data.
- Prototype a shared scene-coordinate environmental-shade approximation. Preserve alpha, homography,
  full/visible masks and legacy behavior. Retain it only after paired review; a negative verdict is valid.
- Retain separate fast/quality recipes and unchanged production defaults. Report costs rather than impose
  a universal slowdown ceiling. Linear-light composition and physical 2D reflectance/contact shadows
  remain deferred.
- Allow up to three additional CC0 grounds, one HDR and two mesh assets. Prefer fixing existing asset
  scale/mapping before expanding. Mesh import is isolated prototype scope, not a core backend.

## Autonomous development and evidence

GPT-6.1 Terra authors/reviews scripted 3D shaders/context and render crops; a fresh reviewer assesses
selected candidates. Freeze baseline and development scenes, change one cue family at a time, allow
up to two adjustment rounds with two candidates each, and record observable gains/regressions without
an aesthetic score. Reserve unseen synthetic scene combinations for final review; these are not real
holdout evidence. Up to two specialist recipes per engine declare goals and deviations explicitly.

Two wider prototypes may test procedural geometry and CC0 contextual meshes in separate worktrees.
Keep their code unpromoted and retain hypotheses, manifests, diagnostics and verdicts for later planning.

The cumulative T4 rendering/probe/warmup/retry budget is 7200 seconds; wider prototypes share at most
1440 seconds (720 each) and final qualification retains at least 1800 seconds. Ledger reservations survive
interruptions conservatively. Reuse existing memory/swap guards; never lower them after a stop. Release
owned remote allocations after verified retrieval. Local short controls and setup are reported separately.

Use 640-square previews and 1280-square/128-sample quality checks. Selected performance confirmation
uses 2 warmups + 20 measured outputs and 3 alternating repeats when budget permits; incomplete cells
remain exploratory. Distinguish accepted output, startup, annotations/retries, encoding/export and
resource costs; do not infer a causal engine multiplier from different hosts or workloads.

Final artifacts: standalone HTML with paired views/crops, coverage/cost/resource figures, compact JSON,
selection/rejection records and reproducible matrices/bundles. Keep raw pools/assets ignored. Require
legacy pixel/trace/identity tests, generic RGBA/family checks, sharp mask/visibility diagnostics,
COCO/evaluator preparation, source/input integrity, schema/package and affected CI checks. Update
roadmap/issues with evidence and explicit gaps; do not close umbrella real-evidence requirements.
