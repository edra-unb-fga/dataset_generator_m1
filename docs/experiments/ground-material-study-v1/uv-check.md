# Ground material and UV: preliminary local evidence

Two CPU renders of an authored, asymmetric directional normal fixture are retained at native resolution: [rotation zero](fixture-0.png) and [rotation 90 degrees](fixture-90.png). Both use one ordinary scene, 640 × 640 pixels, 32 samples, fixed requested and achieved span 1.0, an explicit SUN lamp with angular size 0.00918 radians, and otherwise paired configuration. Both were accepted, report guaranteed ground coverage with no tile clamp, and report 35,054 visible and full mask pixels each. Ground grooves visibly rotate. The visible and full mask arrays compare equal element by element.

The second cell also rotated the light 90 degrees in world space. The coordinate transform maps UV rotation to the inverse texture rotation in world space; the light rotation direction selected in this exploratory pair may consequently be wrong. Comparing rotated whole images after filtering colored pad pixels gave negative correlations (approximately -0.24 and -0.42 in the two image rotation directions). Camera orientation, denoising, object shadows, and the rotation sign prevent a defensible tangent-space correctness conclusion from this pair. A corrected pair and a direct Blender tangent check are described below; the initial pair remains inconclusive.

This local diagnostic preceded the authorized GPU material screen; see the [combined report](index.html). The initial local images predate a later role-aware material preflight change; renderer source was unchanged by that change, but the complete source identity differs. Those broader comparisons are recorded separately in the combined report. Raw configurations, render evidence, guards and the interrupted attempts are under ignored `outputs/experiments/ground-material-20261029`. Fewer than 20 fixtures, no real references, and no matched high-quality reference limit all conclusions.

## Corrected nadir fixture and Blender tangent basis

On the final renderer source, Blender 4.2.1 `mesh.calc_tangents` was called after the actual `ground_mesh_uv` and `ground_uv` functions on a flat plane. All four loops agreed within 1e-5 with tangent/bitangent (+X,+Y) at 0°, and (-Y,+X) at 90°. The source functions were extracted from the actual files and the raw evidence records their hashes. This checks Blender’s derived tangent basis for this plane; it does not by itself qualify arbitrary meshes or all material effects.

A corrected [0° image](nadir-0.png) and [90° image](nadir-90.png) use nadir view, fixed identical 256 × 256 framing, 32 samples, no HDR contribution and one directional sun. UV coordinate rotation of +90° corresponds to -90° world texture rotation, and the sun direction was rotated accordingly. Both renders were accepted, and visible and full mask arrays match exactly. After clockwise rotation of the first image, excluding the union of both target masks and a 16-pixel border, 41,904 remaining ground pixels have mean absolute grayscale difference 0.51 on a 0–255 scale and Pearson correlation 0.981. The opposite rotation gives 4.25 and 0.653. The close matched result, together with Blender’s tangent values, supports the intended UV/tangent behavior for this controlled directional fixture; denoising and quantization remain, and it does not validate all combinations or photographic realism.

The 18-cell planned matrix and its study scripts are retained in `experiments/blenderproc/studies/ground-material-study/`; hashes of the matrix and fixture and controller scripts are saved there. The completed GPU span/material/indoor screen is recorded in the combined report.
