# Coherent-varied: initial 2D screen

The opt-in candidate was compared with `realistic-heavy` on the same production scenes at 640×640. A four-pair-per-family screen and a fresh 20-pair-per-family screen completed with valid pools and matching paired geometry, annotation and full/visible mask signatures. Their local, ignored artifacts are `outputs/experiments/coherent-varied-screen-20261105` and `outputs/experiments/coherent-varied-fresh-20261105`; full images, resolved settings, source hashes and the independent visual reviews remain there. These are not independent source-asset or real-image holdouts.

The independent review of all 60 fresh pairs found coherent foreground/background lighting and useful target-crossing shade, but also a conspicuous straight band and softened small gauge detail in one native-size example. The comparison changes multiple effects at once and cannot isolate their contributions.

A matched replay of manometro slot 16 showed the original candidate applied a four-tap 5×5 motion kernel, while a reduced-motion candidate did not; other final effect activations and scene signatures matched. The replay appeared sharper. The experimental manometro profile reduces motion-blur probability and maximum kernel size, but its 20-pair replay reused the same seed and assets and needs fresh-seed review. The retained local review is `outputs/experiments/coherent-varied-manometro-ablation-20261105/REVIEW.md`.

Three guarded 12-pair landing-pad repetitions yielded baseline 2.084, 1.938 and 2.032 accepted images/s versus candidate 1.449, 1.417 and 1.437 images/s (medians 2.032 and 1.437). These are exploratory: same seed, fixed baseline-first order, no separate warmup and other tests overlapping parts of two repetitions. Peak observed process-tree RSS was 363,315,200 bytes, minimum available system memory 6,292,086,784 bytes, with no observed additional swap-out. Do not treat these as general performance estimates or evidence of detector utility.

No family default is approved or promoted. Candidate shadows, combination-specific softness, diversity and performance require further review.

A subsequent fresh-seed manometro run used the reduced-motion candidate for 20 pairs, with valid pools and matching signatures. Its independent visual review found recognizable dials but difficult small numerals and reduced contrast under some shadows; a rectangular shadow was conspicuous. Motion blur activated in 0/20 candidates, so these images do not assess the reduced 3-pixel blur. The study reuses source assets; see the local `coherent-varied-manometro-fresh-20261105/independent-visual-review.md` for the selection and limits.
