# Coordinated 3D lighting: initial matched screen

An L4 study rendered four matched scenes per context (`open`, `outdoor-night`, `indoor`) with baseline, broad and soft lamp treatments: 36 accepted images. The controller verified archive size and SHA-256, the sealed source and input fingerprints, unchanged scene controls, and exact full/visible masks across triplets. The remote reported completion and the controller confirmed shutdown. The displayed account balance changed from 155.38 to 155.31 CU; lagged balance is not a precise billing measurement. Full images, metrics, provenance and the independent review remain in ignored `outputs/experiments/lighting-round3-reviewed-20261105`; controller artifacts remain in `outputs/experiments/lighting-round3-20261105`.

The independent reviewer inspected all 36 images and selected native-size triplets. Broad lighting generally moderated target washout in inspected indoor and outdoor-night examples, but the brightest indoor case remained pale. The open triplets were visually indistinguishable and sampled no lamps. For indoor 000, the fraction of visible-target pixels with at least one display RGB channel at or above 250 was 37.5%, 29.7% and 33.1% in baseline, broad and soft respectively; this is a display diagnostic, not calibrated clipping or a detector score. Treatments vary several lamp parameters together, so their individual effects cannot be isolated.

The screen used four related compositions per condition, fast renders and flat pad targets. It does not estimate natural-mixture frequencies, validate the changed lamp-type weights or prove realism or detector benefit. A natural-mixture and fresh-seed evaluation remains necessary before any user review of a proposed 3D default.

## Fresh natural mixture

A fresh-seed natural draw of the nine-member candidate produced 24/24 accepted, verified images. The archive, bundle identity, source and input provenance were verified and runtime shutdown confirmed. The run reused the reference assets. Its local artifacts and independent review are in `outputs/experiments/lighting-natural-reviewed-20261105`.

The independent review found several readable targets alongside an indoor scene with nearly invisible markers outside a bright oval, another with a bright patch over a pad, and a large rectangular dark area crossing a target in an open scene. These observations justify investigating resolved settings; unpaired scenes and the small sample do not establish how frequently such cases occur or whether the candidate improves model performance. The natural run's displayed balance after shutdown was 155.11 CU, with delayed provider billing and therefore no exact cost assertion.
